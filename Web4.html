Web 4.0 (Symbiotic Web, conceptual)

Predicted to be a “symbiotic” web combining IoT (Internet of Things), advanced AI, voice/gesture interfaces, and possibly brain-computer interfaces. It implies an intelligent, always-connected environment where digital and physical realities merge (e.g., smart cities, ubiquitous computing). It is the next leap toward ambient intelligence and seamless human-computer symbiosis.
