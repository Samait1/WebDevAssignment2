Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP)

TCP/IP is the fundamental communication protocol suite of the Internet. IP handles addressing and routing (like a postal service delivering packets to the correct address). TCP ensures reliable, ordered, and error-checked delivery of data between applications (like verifying that all pages of a letter arrived intact). It enables different devices and networks to communicate seamlessly, forming the backbone of all Internet data transfer.
