Web 3.0 (Semantic & Decentralized Web, emerging)

Focuses on machine-readable data (Semantic Web), AI-driven personalization, and decentralization via blockchain technologies. Key concepts: self-sovereignty, decentralized storage, and tokens (cryptocurrency, NFTs). It aims to give users ownership of their data and enable trustless, peer-to-peer interactions without central intermediaries.
